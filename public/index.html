About VSSTI

Who We Are & Our Mission

Vector Safety Solutions and Technical Training Institute (VSSTI) is a premier occupational health, safety, and environment (OSH) academic center based in Rawalpindi, Pakistan. We empower organizations and young professionals to meet international safety compliance standards.

Our mission is to foster an ingrained preventive safety culture where zero industrial injuries is an achievable reality. Through hands-on equipment labs, accredited UK & US syllabi, and active job placements, we bridge the gap between classroom theory and heavy industry demands.

Our Core Values

Safety First

Human life and workplace well-being take precedence over all else.

Integrity & Compliance

Innovation
